# Markowitz Finance

**Put your portfolio to work.**

Markowitz Finance brings modern portfolio theory to DeFi on Solana. You pick the assets and their target weights, and your portfolio rebalances itself while it earns trading fees.

## How it works

- **Build your portfolio.** Choose any mix of crypto, stablecoins, commodities and tokenized stocks, with precise target weights.
- **Liquidity does the rebalancing.** Your portfolio is deployed into [Stabble](https://stabble.net) weighted pools (80/20 and 20/80 pairs). When prices move, traders swap against those pools and bring your portfolio back toward its targets. You get paid for it.
- **Earn on everything you hold.** Every swap through your pools pays fees, so the whole portfolio generates yield instead of sitting idle.
- **Rebalance any time.** Change your weights and the app reallocates in a few signatures, with swaps routed through Jupiter.

## Our stack

We run our own infrastructure end to end: an on-chain indexer that tracks every pool, position and reward, portfolio analytics and a Markowitz efficient-frontier analysis, all served from the edge.
